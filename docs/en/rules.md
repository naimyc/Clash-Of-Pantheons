# ⚔️ Rules – Clash of Pantheon

## 🏛️ Game Setup

### 👥 Team Composition
Each player builds a **Pantheon Team** consisting of:

- 👑 **1 Leader God**  
- ✨ **3 Deities**  
- 🛡️ **6 Troups**  
- 🐉 **1 Mythos**

Each **Leader God** has a predefined **Divine Formation**, defining strengths and weaknesses.

---

### 🏆 Trophy Requirements
- 🏙️ **Greek Cities:** 0–500 Trophies  
- 🌑 **Underworld:** 500–1000 Trophies  
- ☁️ **Olympus:** 1000–1500 Trophies  

- Players earn **+10 🏆 Trophies** per victory.

---

# 📜 Game Rules

## 🥇 1. Victory Condition
A player wins when:
- The opponent’s 👑 **Leader God** is defeated, **or**
- Their Leader God has ❤️ **less HP** than yours when the match ends.


## ⚡ 2. Energy System (Divine Energy)
At the start of each round, you gain **3 ⚡ Divine Energy**.

Divine Energy is used for:
- 🚶 Movement  
- 🔮 Skills (**Divine Abilities**)

### 🔥 Special Energy Rules
- If **both players have less than ⏳ 2 minutes remaining**, each gains **5 ⚡ per round**.  
- If you have **less than ⏱️ 20 seconds** and your opponent has **more than ⏳ 2 minutes**, you gain **4 ⚡ per round**.


## ⏱️ 3.Time Rules
- Each round lasts a maximum of **15 seconds**.
- Each player has **5 minutes total time**.


## 🧭 5. Movement Rules
- A unit may only be moved **once per round**.  
- You may **skip a round** to conserve energy 💤.  
- Certain units can move up to **3 tiles per action**.


## 🔋 6. Energy Storage (Aether Reserve)
- You can store up to **10 ⚡ Divine Energy**.  
- Excess energy is transferred into the **🧪 Aether Reserve**.

### 🧪 Aether Reserve Rules
- Stored energy can be used in future rounds.  
- (Optional) May allow special mechanics like 💥 burst usage or delayed regeneration.

## 💸 7. Energy Costs

| Unit Type           | Movement Cost | Ability Cost (Divine Ability) |
|--------------------|-------------|--------------------------------|
| 🛡️ **Pawn**        | 1 ⚡        | — |
| 🐉 **Hero / Monster** | 1 ⚡     | 2 ⚡ |
| ✨ **Minor Gods**   | 1 ⚡        | 4 ⚡ |
| 👑 **Leader God**  | 2 ⚡        | 6 ⚡ |
---

## 📌 Additional Notes
- Some abilities may affect targets within **up to 3 tiles 🎯**.  
- Additional **Pantheon Formations** can be added in future expansions 📦.
