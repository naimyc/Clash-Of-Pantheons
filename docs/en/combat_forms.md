# ⚔️ Combat Forms – Clash of Pantheon

Each unit has exactly one combat form that defines its playstyle.

## ⚔️ 1. Melee
# 👉 Frontline / direct confrontation

### Properties:
- Must engage in direct contact
- High damage in 1v1 situations
- Strong at breaking through lines

### Strengths:
- 💥 High burst damage
- 🧱 Strong for frontline control
- ⚔️ Effective against ranged units

### Weaknesses:
- 🏹 Vulnerable to ranged attacks
- 📏 Highly dependent on positioning

---

## 🏹 2. Ranged
# 👉 Distance / control from the backline

### Properties:
- Attacks from a distance
- Can act across lines (depending on visibility rules)

### Strengths:
- 🎯 Consistent damage output
- 📍 Controls zones
- 🧠 Effective against slow units

### Weaknesses:
- ⚔️ Weak in melee combat
- 🚧 Dependent on protection (frontline)

---

## 🛡️ 3. Defensive (Support / Control)
# 👉 Stabilization & control

### Properties:
- Protects other units
- Blocks or slows enemies
- Deals little direct damage

### Strengths:
- 🧱 High formation stability
- 🌀 Control abilities (slow, block, shield)
- 🛡️ Protects key units

### Weaknesses:
- 💥 Low damage
- 🚶 Limited flexibility

---

## ⚖️ Combat Form Balance (Important)

### Interaction:
- ⚔️ Melee > 🏹 Ranged (when contact is established)
- 🏹 Ranged > ⚔️ Melee (before contact)
- 🛡️ Defensive = Stability (no direct counter, but a support role)
