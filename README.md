# Clash of Pantheons

A turn-based strategy game featuring gods, demigods, and units from various mythologies.

---

## 🧩 Core Idea

- Grid-based battlefield (chess-like)
- Objective: Defeat the enemy god(s)
- Focus on team-building, abilities, and resource management

---

## ⚙️ Features

- ⏱️ Time limit per player (e.g. 5 minutes)
- ⏳ Rounds generates energy
- ⚡ Energy is used for movements/ abilities
- 🔄 Fallen units can be revived using energy (depending on rank)
- 🧠 Each unit has unique abilities and stats
- 🗺️ Different maps / environments
- 📖 Story mode
- ⚔️ PvP & PvE
- 🛠️ Team-building system

---

## 🔁 Game Loop

1. Gain energy  
2. Move units  
3. Attack  
4. Use abilities  

---

## 🧙 Unit Types

### 👑 Leader God (G)
- Example: Zeus, Hades  
- 1 per team  
- Strongest character  
- High ability cost  
- Defines the team archetype  

---

### 🌩️ Minor Gods (U)
- Example: Hermes, Hemera  
- 3 per team
- Have their own abilities  
- Support overall strategy  

---
### 🛡️ Mythos (M)
- Example: Hydra, Medusa, Cerberus
- 1 per team  
- Modify Enviroment
- Freely selectable  

---

### ⚔️ Troops - (P/A)

- Melee / Ranged / Defensive
- Weakest units  
- Low-cost abilities  
- Quantity and type depend on chosen god  

---

## ⚡ Resource System

- Energy increases over rounds
- Used for:
  - Abilities  
  - Reviving units 

---

## 🧠 Strategy Elements

- Team synergies based on chosen god  
- Grid positioning  
- Ability timing  
- Resource management  

---
